IS LITECLIP SAFE?  (no tech knowledge needed)
==============================================

LiteClip is a free clipping app: press Alt+X and it saves the last 15 seconds,
30 seconds or 1 minute (or any length you set) of your screen and game sound.
That's all it does. Your microphone is only recorded if you turn it on in
Settings.

You don't have to trust anyone. Here's how to check it yourself in 2 minutes:


1) LET 70 ANTIVIRUS PROGRAMS CHECK IT
   - Go to  www.virustotal.com
   - Drag  LiteClip.exe  onto the page (or click "Choose file").
   - You'll see how many of ~70 antivirus programs (Microsoft, Kaspersky,
     Bitdefender, Norton, ...) found anything.

   Note: brand-new apps from small creators sometimes get 1 or 2 flags from
   small, over-careful scanners, just because the app is new and not "signed"
   (signing costs money every year). What matters: the big names are clean.


2) SCAN IT WITH WINDOWS' OWN ANTIVIRUS
   - Right-click the LiteClip folder  ->  "Scan with Microsoft Defender".


3) SIGNS ANYONE CAN SEE THAT IT'S HARMLESS
   - It never asks for admin rights (no "Do you want to allow this app to
     make changes?" pop-up).
   - There's no installer. It's just this folder. Settings -> "Remove LiteClip"
     deletes everything it made (your clips are kept).
   - No ads, no account, nothing to sign up for. It's light too: about 4 MB of
     memory while it waits in the tray.
   - It doesn't use the internet. You can block it in Windows Firewall and it
     keeps working exactly the same.
   - It only saves things in: Videos\LiteClip (your clips), a temp folder for
     the last few minutes of recording, and a small settings file.
   - Settings -> Advanced shows the exact command it uses to record. Nothing hidden.


"WINDOWS PROTECTED YOUR PC" ?
   Windows shows this blue warning for every new app that isn't signed with a
   paid certificate, not because something is wrong. Click  "More info"  then
   "Run anyway".


WHAT'S IN THIS FOLDER
   LiteClip.exe          the app
   tools\ffmpeg.exe      the recording engine (FFmpeg, the same free engine
   tools\ffprobe.exe     used by OBS, VLC and YouTube; official version)
   source\               the full source code, for anyone who wants to read it
                         or build the app themselves
   lang\                 translations (plain text files you can open in Notepad)
   FINGERPRINTS.txt      unique "fingerprints" of each file, so you can confirm
                         nobody changed them (they're also on the GitHub release
                         page, which GitHub builds in public from the source)


HOW TO USE
   1. Double-click LiteClip.exe. A window opens, and LiteClip starts recording
      in the background (white ring with a red dot near your clock).
   2. In a game, press Alt+X to save the last 30 seconds (change the length
      to 15s or 1 min in the window).
   3. Your clips are in  Videos\LiteClip. Open LiteClip to watch and trim them.
   4. Open Settings in LiteClip to change anything: shortcuts, clip lengths,
      quality, microphone, file names, colours and more. README.md lists
      every option.

   Want it on a USB stick? Settings -> Advanced -> Portable mode.

   To remove it: Settings -> "Remove LiteClip from this PC". It asks
   "Are you sure? It's permanent." and then deletes everything it made.
   Your clips stay. Then delete LiteClip.exe yourself.
