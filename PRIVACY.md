# Privacy

**LiteClip collects nothing.**

- It has no internet code at all: no analytics, no telemetry, no update checks, no crash reports, no accounts.
  GitHub checks this on every change (see `.github/workflows/build.yml`), and you can block LiteClip in
  Windows Firewall without losing anything.
- Your recordings stay on your PC. LiteClip never uploads, shares or sends them anywhere.
  *Copy* and *Fit for Discord* only put the file on your clipboard; you decide where to paste it.
- Your microphone is **off by default**. When it's on, it can be muted with a shortcut.
- The rolling recording is kept in small temporary files that are constantly overwritten.
- The only files LiteClip writes are listed in the README ("What LiteClip writes to your PC"), and
  *Settings → Remove LiteClip* deletes all of them.
